This project develops a MATLAB/Simulink simulation of a permanent magnet synchronous motor (PMSM) drive supplied through a three-phase inverter. The model includes a DC source, the inverter with PWM switching, the PMSM and its sensors, and is controlled with field-oriented control: decoupling, PI current loops for the d and q axes, and an outer PI speed loop. The aim is to evaluate speed-reference tracking and load-disturbance rejection, and to compare the results with a simpler dq-frame model and a published simulation (Tripathi et al., 2023). It is a self-directed, simulation-only study, and its assumptions and limitations are stated in the report.
